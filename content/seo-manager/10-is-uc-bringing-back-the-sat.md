# Is UC Bringing Back the SAT?

"Is SAT prep worth it?" is one of the top questions parents search. For California families, it now comes with a second question: *will the UC system require the SAT again?*

Here is what is confirmed, what isn't, and what a Tri-Valley high schooler should do this year.

## What's confirmed (as of September 2026)

- **UC does not currently use SAT/ACT scores in admissions.** The UC Board of Regents voted in 2020 to eliminate them.
- **A formal review is underway.** UC's Academic Senate has committed to complete a review of standardized testing in admissions **by June 2027** (CalMatters, July 2026).
- **Only the Regents can reinstate a test requirement.** The Academic Senate can only recommend.
- **Many colleges outside the UC system require scores again.** As of fall 2027 applications, all eight Ivy League schools plus MIT, Stanford, Georgetown, Johns Hopkins, Carnegie Mellon, Rice, Vanderbilt, UT Austin and others require the SAT or ACT (The College Investor, Sept 2026).
- **CSU** dropped SAT/ACT from admissions in 2022. Check CSU's current policy before applying.

## What's not confirmed

Whether UC will bring the SAT back, and for which graduating class. Anyone claiming a firm date is guessing. **Plan for both outcomes.**

## Common signs parents notice

- Your student has strong grades but hasn't tried a full, timed practice test.
- PSAT scores didn't match classroom grades.
- Your student plans to apply to at least one test-required college.
- Math sections feel rushed; reading sections feel "fine" but scores are average.

## What may actually be happening

A gap between grades and test scores usually isn't about test tricks. It's often a **learning gap** that classwork didn't expose: a shaky algebra skill, slow reading with evidence, or a repeated **mistake pattern** under time pressure.

## Is SAT prep worth it?

It's worth it when **any** of these are true:

- Your student will apply to one or more test-required or test-recommended colleges.
- They want to be considered for merit scholarships (some schools use scores even when test-optional).
- Their practice score is below the typical range for their target schools.
- A diagnostic shows specific, fixable gaps.

It's less urgent if your student is applying only to test-blind schools. But because UC's policy is under review, many families prepare anyway so scores are ready if needed.

## FAQ

**Is UC bringing back the SAT?**
Not yet. UC's Academic Senate is reviewing standardized testing, with a review due by June 2027. Only the UC Board of Regents can reinstate a requirement.

**Does UC accept SAT scores in 2026?**
UC does not use SAT or ACT scores in admissions decisions under its current policy. Check UC admissions for updates, since a review is underway.

**Which colleges require the SAT again?**
For fall 2027 applicants, all eight Ivy League schools, MIT, Stanford, Georgetown, Johns Hopkins, Carnegie Mellon and others require the SAT or ACT. Always confirm on each college's admissions site.

**Is SAT prep worth it if UC doesn't require it?**
Yes if your student applies to any test-required college or wants merit scholarship consideration. It's less urgent for students applying only to test-blind schools.

**When should my child start SAT prep?**
Many students start in the summer before or fall of junior year, after a diagnostic shows where points are being lost.
